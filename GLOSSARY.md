# Moments

Keeping the parts of videos you love, to replay them on loop.

## Language

**Moment**:
A stretch of a Source between a start and an end, saved to replay. It stands on its own: it keeps playing after its Source is gone.
_Avoid_: Cut, clip, highlight, segment

**Source**:
The full video a Moment is taken from, such as a movie or a YouTube video.
_Avoid_: Original, input, media

**Library**:
All of your Moments.
_Avoid_: Collection, gallery

**Queue**:
Moments lined up to play one after another.
_Avoid_: Playlist, reel

**Trimmer**:
Where you watch a Source and mark the start and end of new Moments.
_Avoid_: Editor, cutter

**Save**:
A Moment on its way into the Library, from pressing Save in the Trimmer until its file is in the Moments folder. A failed Save stays until it's retried or dismissed.
_Avoid_: Job, export, render, save queue
