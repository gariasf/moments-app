# A Moment is identified by the instant it was saved

A Moment's identity is the `creation_time` its Save writes into the file, to the microsecond. A play log, a share link and a re-trim each have to find a Moment after its title or its file changes, and the Library has no database (ADR-0002). Every Moment already carries that instant, Saves run one at a time so no two share it, and it travels with the file through a rename, a move out and back, or a copy made in Files. A re-trim writes the old instant into the new file, so a re-trimmed Moment keeps its date and its place in the Library. A random id in its own tag would keep identity apart from the date, but every Moment saved before it would need its file rewritten, or the app a second rule for files without one.

What follows is deliberate. A file the app didn't save carries no `moments.*` tags and has no identity: it shows and plays, with no plays counted and no share links, and the app never writes into it. Two files with the same instant are the same Moment twice: both show, they share plays and links, and nothing reconciles them, even after one is re-trimmed. The app still addresses a Moment by its title; the identity goes only into records that must outlive a rename.
