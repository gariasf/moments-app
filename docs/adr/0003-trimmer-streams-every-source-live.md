# The Trimmer streams every Source through a live rewrap

The Trimmer never plays a Source file as it is, even when the browser could. The Umbrel streams the Source from wherever you seek as fragmented MP4: the video is copied, and the chosen audio track is copied or converted as a save would. A small player in the page feeds that stream to MediaSource. Most Sources won't play as they are. Safari can't open MKV or HEVC tagged `hev1`, and Chrome plays AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS and TrueHD silent and can't switch audio tracks. With one path, the preview carries the streams the Moment will get, at the Source's own timestamps. The alternatives each cost more: HLS needs a keyframe index of the whole Source before it can play, plus hls.js; rewrapping the whole Source first means minutes of waiting; and a proxy isn't what the Moment gets. The price is a hand-written MSE player, and a stream that needs exact ffmpeg flags to keep the Source's timestamps.
